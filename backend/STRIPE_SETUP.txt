IronFit - Educational Stripe EUR integration

TEST ONLY. No registered French business is being claimed. sk_live_ and live
webhook events remain rejected. No live activation is configured or authorized.
The configured French test account supports EUR; this does not establish live eligibility.

Approved new membership prices: Séance EUR 10; Mensuel EUR 60;
Trimestriel EUR 160; Annuel EUR 550. These are explicit user-approved prices,
not a currency conversion. On startup existing matching TND plans are updated
once to these approved EUR prices. Other legacy plans retain TND and cannot
be purchased via Stripe until an EUR price is explicitly approved/configured.
New plans created by the admin use EUR.

Restart the backend from backend/ so .env is loaded. Development DDL_AUTO=update
adds currency to plans, payments, stripe_orders and amount_minor to payments.
Legacy currency defaults to TND; original payment amounts are preserved.
Legacy payment minor amounts are backfilled as millimes, EUR uses integer cents.
Revenue totals are grouped by currency. Merchandise prices remain TND, since
no EUR merchandise prices were approved.

STRIPE_SECRET_KEY=sk_test_... (never commit or expose)
STRIPE_TEST_MODE=true (false rejected)
STRIPE_CURRENCY=eur (other new-payment currencies rejected)
STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET=whsec_... from the real local listener/endpoint
STRIPE_RETURN_URL=http://localhost:5173 (set to the actual frontend port)
STRIPE_AUTOMATIC_TAX=false until test Tax configuration is ready

Install official Stripe CLI https://docs.stripe.com/stripe-cli and authenticate
with the SAME test account. Keep this running:
stripe listen --forward-to localhost:8080/api/payments/stripe/webhook
Put its signing secret in .env then restart backend. API keys and webhook signing
secrets are different. Never fabricate a signing secret.
Listen for checkout.session.completed, checkout.session.expired,
checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded, checkout.session.async_payment_failed,
invoice.paid, invoice.payment_failed, customer.subscription.updated/deleted.

One-time Checkout has invoice_creation enabled. Subscriptions create invoices
in the currency of their EUR prices, and renewal uses that subscription currency.
Configure recurrence only for actual plan IDs via STRIPE_PLAN_N_INTERVAL and
STRIPE_PLAN_N_INTERVAL_COUNT. Legacy Stripe subscriptions and orders are not
repriced or converted: they retain original currency and fulfillment semantics.
A new EUR subscription must be purchased separately after managing any old one.
Use the test customer portal for cancellation/payment-method updates; do not
enable plan switching, trials or proration without corresponding access policies.

For Stripe Tax configure the account test tax origin/registrations and real plan
tax codes STRIPE_PLAN_N_TAX_CODE; then enable STRIPE_AUTOMATIC_TAX. Prices are
exclusive of configured taxes. No fixed tax percentage is invented.

Verify a successful checkout with 4242 4242 4242 4242 (test card only), a declined
card and cancellation; inspect the EUR invoice, verified membership activation,
recurring invoice renewal, and unchanged TND history. Signed automated fixtures
are regression coverage and do not replace actual end-to-end Stripe testing.
